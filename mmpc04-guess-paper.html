Unit 6: Understanding and Classifying Costs

Cost is the value of economic resources sacrificed to achieve a specific objective. Cost accounting systematically records, classifies, and analyses costs for control and decision-making.

Unit 7: Absorption and Marginal Costing

Absorption Costing includes all manufacturing costs (fixed + variable) in product cost. Marginal Costing includes only variable costs; fixed costs are treated as period costs.

Unit 8: Activity Based Costing (ABC)

Activity Based Costing (ABC) allocates overheads based on activities that drive costs, not volume-based measures. Core principle: "Activities consume resources; products consume activities."

Unit 9: Cost-Volume-Profit (CVP) Analysis

CVP Analysis examines the relationship between costs, volume, and profit. It determines the break-even point, margin of safety, and target profit sales.

Unit 10: Budgeting and Budgetary Control

A budget is a quantitative expression of a plan for a defined period. Budgetary control is the system of comparing actual results with budgets and taking corrective action.

Unit 11: Variance Analysis

Variance Analysis compares standard (budgeted) costs with actual costs to identify deviations. Favourable (F): Actual < Standard. Adverse (A): Actual > Standard.

Unit 13: Comparative, Common Size and Trend Statements
