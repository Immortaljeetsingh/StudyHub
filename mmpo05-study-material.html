Unit 1: Logistics and Supply Chain Management — An Introduction

Logistics = management of flow of goods from origin to consumption. Supply Chain = network of organizations involved in producing and delivering products from raw material to end customer. SCM = coordination of supply chain activities to maximize customer value and achieve sustainable competitive advantage. PDM = management.

Customer Value = Benefits received minus Costs incurred (including price, time, effort). Order Qualifiers = minimum requirements to even be considered by customers. SCOR Model (Supply Chain Operations Reference) = Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return. Push System = produce based on forecasts; Pull System = produce based on actual demand. Leagile = combination of lean (upstream) and agile (downstream).
