Unit 1: Introduction to IMC

📖 Definition

IMC (Kotler & Armstrong): A company carefully integrates and coordinates its many communication channels to deliver a clear, consistent, and compelling message.

Promotions Mix: The specific blend of advertising, PR, personal selling, sales promotion, and direct marketing tools the company uses to pursue its marketing objectives.

📊 IMC Process — TAPIC
Target Market Segments
↓
Ascertain Communication Objectives
↓
Plan and Design Messages
↓
Implement Promotional Strategy
↓
Collect Feedback
AdvertisingPRPersonal SellingSales PromotionDirect MarketingDigital MarketingGuerrilla Marketing
💡 Real-World Example

Amul's cookie launch used social media contests, comparative content (25% vs 2% butter), and hashtag campaigns — achieving massive awareness with zero advertising spend.

🧠 Memory Aid

TAPIC = Target, Ascertain, Plan, Implement, Collect

APPS-DDG = Advertising, PR, Personal Selling, Sales Promotion, Direct, Digital, Guerrilla

✅ Unit Summary

  • IMC = Coordination of all communication tools for clear, consistent message
  • 7 Promotions Mix tools: APPS-DDG
  • IMC Process: TAPIC (5 steps)
  • Types of Advertising: IPRI (Informative, Persuasive, Reminder, Institutional)

Unit 3: Campaign Planning — Strategic Considerations

📖 Definition

A campaign is a planned series of communication steps built around a single theme. Campaigns can be local, regional, national, or international and must align with overall marketing strategy.

📊 Campaign Planning Steps
1. Define Objectives & Target Audience
↓
2. Determine Budget
↓
3. Develop Creative Strategy
↓
4. Select Media & Execute
↓
5. Evaluate & Optimize
💡 Real-World Example

Fevicol's "Stuck by Fevicol" contest asked consumers to create video ads conveying brand message — generating creative content and organic reach at minimal cost.

✅ Unit Summary

  • 6 Campaign Components: Advertising, DM, Personal selling, Sales promo, Digital, PR
  • 7 Planning Steps from objectives to evaluation
  • Campaign types: Consumer-focused, trade-focused, product-specific

Unit 4: Campaign Planning — Creative Considerations

📖 Definition

Product positioning defines how the product should be perceived. Creative brief is the foundational document guiding all creative development. Ad appeals trigger emotional or rational responses.

📊 Advertising Appeals — REM
Rational — Facts, features, benefits
↓
Emotional — Feelings, desires, aspirations
↓
Moral — Ethical, social responsibility
💡 Real-World Example

Surf Excel's "Daag Acche Hain" uses emotional appeal with slice-of-life execution — children getting dirty to help others.

🧠 Memory Aid

REM = Rational, Emotional, Moral (advertising appeals)

SLFMMPETS = Slice of Life, Lifestyle, Fantasy, Mood, Musical, Personality, Technical, Scientific, Testimonial (execution styles)

✅ Unit Summary

  • 3 Advertising Appeals: REM
  • 9 Execution Styles: SLFMMPETS
  • Always pair the right appeal with the right execution style

Unit 5: Campaign Planning — Media Considerations

📖 Definition

Reach: Number of unique people exposed to the ad. Frequency: Average exposures per person. GRP: Reach × Frequency. CPM: Cost to reach 1,000 people.

📊 Media Scheduling Patterns — CPFS
Continuous: Steady throughout the year
↓
Pulsating: Heavy periods alternate with light
↓
Flighting: Heavy then complete stops
↓
Seasonal: Concentrated during peak demand
💡 Real-World Example

IPL advertisers use pulsating schedule — heavy during tournament season, minimal off-season.

🧠 Memory Aid

CPFS = Continuous, Pulsating, Flighting, Seasonal

✅ Unit Summary

  • 5 Media types: TV, Radio, Print, Outdoor, Digital
  • 4 Scheduling patterns: CPFS
  • Key metrics: Reach, Frequency, GRP, CPM

Unit 6: Measuring Advertising Effectiveness

📖 Definition

Effectiveness measurement happens at three stages: pre-testing (focus groups, concept testing), during campaign (reach, frequency, awareness), and post-testing (recall, recognition, sales).

📊 Measurement Framework
Pre-testing: Message clarity, appeal, comprehension
↓
During: Reach, frequency, GRP, awareness
↓
Post-testing: Recall, recognition, attitude change, sales
💡 Real-World Example

Nielsen ratings measure TV GRP; Google Analytics tracks digital reach and frequency.

🧠 Memory Aid

RFG-CPM = Reach, Frequency, GRP, Cost Per Mille, Recall, Recognition

✅ Unit Summary

  • 3 Measurement stages: Pre, During, Post
  • Unaided recall is more valuable than aided recall
  • GRP = Reach × Frequency

Unit 7: Managing Sales Promotion

📖 Definition

Sales promotion = short-term marketing strategies using unique tactics to entice customer action. Requires both a timing restriction and an activity requirement. Differs from advertising: advertising builds brand, promotions drive immediate action.

📊 5 Purposes of Sales Promotion — CISDB
Creating Product Awareness
↓
Interesting the Customer
↓
Stimulating Demand
↓
Delivering Information
↓
Brand Reinforcement
💡 Real-World Example

Amul Butter Cookies launch — social media contest comparing 25% vs 2% butter content created massive organic awareness with zero ad spend.

🧠 Memory Aid

CISDB = Create awareness, Interest, Stimulate demand, Deliver info, Brand reinforcement

✅ Unit Summary

  • 5 Purposes: CISDB
  • 11 Consumer promotion types: Coupons, Rebates, Pricing, Trade-In, Loyalty, Sampling, Free Product, Premiums, Contests, Demos, Appearances
  • Trade promotions: Allowances, contests, co-op ads, POP displays
  • Coupons = immediate savings; Rebates = post-purchase

📊 Direct Marketing Tools — DCTDEM

↓
Telemarketing & Direct Response Ads
↓
Email Marketing & Mobile Marketing
💡 Real-World Example

Amazon's recommendation engine uses purchase history for personalized email and app notifications — "Customers who bought X also bought Y."

🧠 Memory Aid

✅ Unit Summary

  • 6 DM Tools: DCTDEM

Unit 9: Publicity and Public Relations

📖 Definition

PR manages relationships with publics to build favorable image. Earned media has higher credibility than paid advertising. Crisis communication is a critical PR function.

📊 PR Tools — PERC-CL
Press Releases & Events
↓
Relations (Community) & CSR
↓
Crisis Communication & Lobbying
💡 Real-World Example

Tata Group's CSR activities (Tata Trusts) build enormous brand goodwill without direct advertising.

🧠 Memory Aid

PERC-CL = Press Releases, Events, Relations, CSR, Crisis, Lobbying

✅ Unit Summary

  • PR = Earned media, higher credibility, less control
  • Advertising = Paid media, lower credibility, full control
  • 6 PR Tools: PERC-CL

Unit 11: Media Buying — Changing Paradigms

📖 Definition

Media buying has shifted from manual negotiation to automated/programmatic. Real-time bidding allows precise audience targeting. New formats (native, CTV, podcasts) are growing rapidly.

📊 Emerging Media Trends
Programmatic Advertising & RTB
↓
Native Ads & Influencer Partnerships
↓
Connected TV (CTV) & Podcast Ads
💡 Real-World Example

Spotify's programmatic audio ads target listeners by mood, activity, and location.

✅ Unit Summary

  • Programmatic = automated ad buying using algorithms
  • RTB = auction-based ad placement in milliseconds
  • New formats: Native, Influencer, CTV, Podcast
  • Media Buying Process: Define audience → Set objectives → Select vehicles → Negotiate → Monitor

Unit 12: IMC — Legal and Ethical Issues

📖 Definition

Advertising must be truthful, non-deceptive, and substantiated. Self-regulation through ASCI supplements legal framework. Digital advertising raises new privacy and data concerns.

📊 Legal Framework
ASCI — Self-regulatory body for ad content
↓
Consumer Protection Act, 2019
↓
IT Act & IP Laws
💡 Real-World Example

ASCI flagged multiple ads during IPL for misleading claims — showing self-regulation in action.

✅ Unit Summary

  • ASCI = Advertising Standards Council of India (self-regulatory)
  • Ethical issues: Deceptive ads, puffery, privacy, targeting vulnerable groups, subliminal messages
  • Legal compliance vs ethical responsibility

Unit 13: Consumer Movement in India

📖 Definition

Consumer protection is both a legal right and a marketing responsibility. IMC must respect consumer rights in all communications. Digital commerce has added new dimensions to consumer protection.

📊 6 Consumer Rights — SICCHR
Safety & Information
↓
Choice & Hearing
↓
Redressal & Consumer Education
💡 Real-World Example

Maggi noodles ban (2015) demonstrated consequences of misleading labeling — "No added MSG" when MSG was present.

🧠 Memory Aid

SICCHR = Safety, Information, Choice, Hearing, Redressal, Consumer Education

✅ Unit Summary

  • 6 Consumer Rights: SICCHR
  • CPA 2019: Three-tier dispute resolution (District → State → National)
  • E-commerce regulations and product liability provisions
  • All IMC claims must be truthful and non-misleading

Unit 14: Strategies for Integrating IMC Elements

📖 Definition

Integration requires consistency across all touchpoints. Customer journey mapping guides channel selection. Internal alignment ensures every department delivers the same message.

📊 7-Step IMC Planning Framework
1. Situation Analysis
↓
2. Target Audience
↓
3. Objectives & 4. Budget
↓
5. Strategy & 6. Implementation
↓
