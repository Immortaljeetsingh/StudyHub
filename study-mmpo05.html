Block-01: Fundamentals of Logistics & SCM

Unit 1: Logistics and Supply Chain Management — An Introduction

📖 Definition

Logistics = management of flow of goods from origin to consumption. Supply Chain = network of organizations involved in producing and delivering products from raw material to end customer. SCM = coordination of supply chain activities to maximize customer value and achieve sustainable competitive advantage. PDM = managing movement of finished goods from manufacturer to customer. Integrated Logistics = two interrelated efforts — logistics operations (physical distribution, materials management, inventory transfer) and logistics coordination (forecasting, order processing, MRP). Evolution: Physical Distribution → Logistics Management → Supply Chain Management.

Unit 2: Customer Focus in Supply Chain Management

📖 Definition

Customer Value = Benefits received minus Costs incurred (including price, time, effort). Order Qualifiers = minimum requirements to even be considered by customers. Order Winners = features that actually win customer orders over competitors. Customer Retention = keeping existing customers — 5x cheaper than acquisition. A 5% increase in retention can increase profits by 25-95%.

Unit 7: Cost Analysis and Measurement

📖 Definition

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) = purchase price + all operating costs over lifecycle. Activity-Based Costing (ABC) = assigns costs to activities based on resource consumption. Cost Trade-offs = saving in one area may increase costs in another. Logistics Cost Components = transportation (35-50%), warehousing (20-25%), inventory carrying (15-25% of inventory value), administration (5-10%).

📋 Key Formulas Quick Reference

  • TCO = Purchase Price + Operating Costs + Maintenance + Disposal
  • Inventory Turnover = COGS / Average Inventory
  • Fill Rate = Orders filled from stock / Total orders
  • On-time Delivery = On-time orders / Total orders
  • Cash-to-Cash Cycle = Inventory Days + Receivable Days – Payable Days
