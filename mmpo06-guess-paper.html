Unit 1: Introduction to Materials Management

Materials Management = planning, organizing, and controlling material flow from source to user. Materials = one of the 5Ms: Men, Machine, Methods, Money, Materials . Average material cost = 50-70% of total manufacturing expenditure.

Unit 2: Strategic Role of Materials Management

Value Analysis = improving value by reducing cost or improving function. Make or Buy Decision = produce in-house vs outsource. Strategic importance: materials management directly impacts profitability.

Unit 3: Designing Supplier Network

SRM (Supplier Relationship Management) = strategic approach to managing relationships with suppliers for mutual benefit. Supplier selection criteria: Quality, Price, Delivery, Service, Financial stability.

Unit 4: Dynamics of Buyer-Seller Relationships

Partnership Sourcing = long-term relationship between buyer and supplier based on trust, shared goals, and mutual benefit. Negotiation approaches: Distributive (win-lose) vs Integrative (win-win).

Unit 5: Materials Planning and Budgeting

MRP = Material Requirements Planning — determining what, when, and how much to order. BOM = Bill of Materials — hierarchical list of components needed to make a product.

Unit 6: Push and Pull Systems

Push System = production based on forecasts (MRP). Pull System = production based on actual demand (JIT/Kanban). Kanban = visual signal system for pull production. CONWIP = hybrid system with constant WIP.

Unit 7: Work-in-Process (WIP) Inventory

WIP = partially completed products in the production process. Little's Law = WIP = Throughput x Flow Time. WIP causes: batch processing, waiting times, quality issues.

Unit 8: Spare Parts Management

Insurance Spares = parts kept for emergencies. Consignment Stock = supplier owns stock at buyer's location. VMI = Vendor Managed Inventory for spares.

Unit 9: Codification and Standardization

ABC Analysis = classify by value (A=20% items/80% value). VED = Vital, Essential, Desirable (by criticality). FSN = Fast, Slow, Non-moving (by movement). XYZ = by regularity of demand.

Unit 10: Location and Layout of Warehouse

WMS = Warehouse Management System software. Warehouse functions: Receiving, Storage, Picking, Shipping. Layout should minimize travel distance and maximize space utilization.

Unit 11: Warehouse Risk Management

Cycle Counting = regular counting of a small portion of inventory on a rotating basis. FEFO = First Expiry First Out. Risks: fire, theft, damage, obsolescence.

Unit 12: Materials Management Organization

Centralized = all purchasing under one department. Decentralized = each unit manages its own materials. Hybrid = central policy with local execution.
