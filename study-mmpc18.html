Unit 1: Introduction to Entrepreneurship

📖 Core Concepts
  • Entrepreneurship = capacity to assume risk, accountability & challenges to explore opportunities, disrupt market norms & create values
  • Evolution: Rational Agent (Economics) → Personality Traits (Psychology) → Opportunity Recognition (Late 1990s)
  • Three eras of entrepreneurship thinking: risk-bearing, trait-based, opportunity-based

📊 Entrepreneur vs Intrapreneur

💡 Real-World Example

Elon Musk = Entrepreneur (SpaceX, Tesla — own ventures, personal risk). Sundar Pichai = Intrapreneur (innovated within Google — Gmail, Chrome — company resources).

🧠 Memory Aid

"C-S-S-G-K-S" — Cantillon (Risk), Say (Coordination), Schumpeter (Innovation), Gartner (Organizations), Kirzner (Discovery), Shane (Opportunity)

🎯 Exam Tip

Schumpeter's "creative destruction" is the most frequently tested concept. Always cite him first in any entrepreneurship definition question.

✅ Unit Summary

  • Entrepreneurship = risk + opportunity + value creation
  • Schumpeter's "creative destruction" = innovation destroying old equilibrium
  • Entrepreneur bears risk & seeks opportunities; Manager avoids risk & manages resources
  • Intrapreneur = entrepreneur within existing organization

RRearrangeNetflix — watch anytime vs TV schedule
📖 Break-Even Point (BEP) Formula

BEP = Fixed Cost / (Market Price per unit - Variable Cost per unit)

BEP = FC / (MP - VC)

Worked Example: FC = ₹36,20,000; MP = ₹440/unit; VC = ₹40/unit
BEP = 36,20,000 / (440 - 40) = 36,20,000 / 400 = 9,050 units
Annual demand = 10,000 > BEP → Sustainable proposal ✓

📊 BEP Analysis
Revenue (MP × Q) — Profit Zone
↑ Break-Even Point
Total Cost (FC + VC × Q) — Loss Zone
📖 SWOT Analysis
FactorInternal/ExternalFocus
StrengthsInternalWhat you do well
WeaknessesInternalWhere you lack
OpportunitiesExternalMarket openings
ThreatsExternalCompetitive/environmental risks
💡 Real-World Example

Zomato applied SCAMPER — Combined food ordering + technology + delivery. Uber Eats Adapted the ride-sharing model to food delivery. Swiggy Modified the approach by focusing on logistics-first.

🧠 Memory Aid

"SCAMPER = S-C-A-M-P-E-R" — Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to use, Eliminate, Rearrange

🎯 Exam Tip

BEP formula is the #1 formula to memorize. Practice 2-3 numerical problems. SCAMPER and SWOT are also very frequently asked frameworks.

✅ Unit Summary

  • SCAMPER = 7-step idea generation framework
  • BEP = FC / (MP - VC) — where revenue = cost
  • SWOT for scanning and screening business ideas
  • MVP = minimum features, maximum learning

Unit 9: Implementing Business Plan

📖 Implementation Steps (L-L-O-F-I)
  • Location Selection — proximity to raw materials, market, labor, transportation
  • Layout Planning — efficient workflow design (process, product, cellular, fixed-position)
  • Operations Planning — production scheduling, quality control, inventory management
  • Financer Selection — choose between debt/equity based on cost of capital
  • Implementation Schedule — Gantt chart, milestones, deadlines
📖 Common Errors in Business Plan
  • Overoptimistic revenue projections
  • Ignoring competition analysis
  • Underestimating costs (hidden costs)
  • Poor market research methodology
  • Lack of contingency planning (no Plan B)
💡 Real-World Example

Cafe Coffee Day (CCD) expanded aggressively without adequate contingency planning — took on ₹7,000 crore debt for rapid expansion. When coffee prices fell and competition increased, the company couldn't service debt.

🧠 Memory Aid

"L-L-O-F-I" — Location, Layout, Operations, Financer, Implementation schedule

✅ Unit Summary

  • Implementation = turning plan into reality
  • 5 steps: Location → Layout → Operations → Financer → Schedule
  • Common error: Lack of contingency planning

Unit 14: Family Business and Governance

📖 Challenges in Family Business
ChallengeDescriptionImpact
Succession PlanningWho takes over next?Leadership vacuum
Family vs Business ConflictPersonal vs professional decisionsPoor decision-making
ProfessionalizationHiring non-family managersResistance from family
Governance IssuesLack of formal structuresAccountability gaps
📖 Coping Strategies
  • Clear Governance Structure: Board of directors, advisory board
  • Professional Management: Hire qualified non-family executives
  • Family Constitution: Written rules for family-business interaction
  • Conflict Resolution: Mediation, regular family meetings
  • Merit-based Promotions: Performance evaluation, not just family ties
💡 Real-World Example

Reliance Industries — Dhirubhai Ambani founded, Mukesh & Anil took over. The split demonstrated both succession challenges and governance issues. Tata Group = better governance model with professional management + family oversight.

🧠 Memory Aid

"S-F-P-G-C" — Succession, Family conflict, Professionalization, Governance, Constitution

🎯 Exam Tip

Succession planning is the #1 topic in family business questions. Compare Reliance (conflict) vs Tata (smooth governance) as contrasting examples.

✅ Unit Summary

  • Family businesses = majority of Indian enterprises
  • Key challenges: Succession, Family-Business conflict, Professionalization
  • Solution: Family Constitution + Professional Management + Governance Board
