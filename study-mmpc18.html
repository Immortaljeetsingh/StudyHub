Block-01: Entrepreneurship — An Overview

Unit 1: Introduction to Entrepreneurship

📖 Core Concepts
  • Entrepreneurship = capacity to assume risk, accountability & challenges to explore opportunities, disrupt market norms & create values
  • Evolution: Rational Agent (Economics) → Personality Traits (Psychology) → Opportunity Recognition (Late 1990s)
  • Three eras of entrepreneurship thinking: risk-bearing, trait-based, opportunity-based

🎯 Exam Tip

Schumpeter's "creative destruction" is the most frequently tested concept. Always cite him first in any entrepreneurship definition question.

📐 Case Study: Dhirubhai Ambani — India's Greatest Entrepreneur

Dhirubhai Ambani started Reliance Industries with ₹15,000 in 1966. He demonstrated every entrepreneurial trait: risk-bearing (entered petrochemicals when India was socialist), opportunity recognition (saw India's polyester demand), innovation (backward integration strategy), and creative destruction (disrupted the textile industry). From a small textile mill to a ₹15 lakh crore empire — the ultimate entrepreneurship story.

✅ Unit Summary

  • Entrepreneurship = risk + opportunity + value creation
  • Schumpeter's "creative destruction" = innovation destroying old equilibrium
  • Entrepreneur bears risk & seeks opportunities; Manager avoids risk & manages resources
  • Intrapreneur = entrepreneur within existing organization

📖 BEP Formula

BEP = Fixed Cost / (Market Price per unit - Variable Cost per unit)

Worked Example: FC = ₹36,20,000; MP = ₹440/unit; VC = ₹40/unit
BEP = 36,20,000 / (440 - 40) = 36,20,000 / 400 = 9,050 units
Annual demand = 10,000 > BEP → Sustainable proposal ✓

📐 Case Study: Zomato — SCAMPER in Action

Zomato applied SCAMPER — Combined food ordering + technology + delivery. Uber Eats Adapted the ride-sharing model to food delivery. Swiggy Modified the approach by focusing on logistics-first. Each startup used different SCAMPER elements to innovate in the same space.

🧠 Memory Aid

"SCAMPER = S-C-A-M-P-E-R" — Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to use, Eliminate, Rearrange

✅ Unit Summary

  • SCAMPER = 7-step idea generation framework
  • SWOT = Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
  • BEP = FC / (MP - VC) — #1 formula to memorize
  • MVP = minimum features, maximum learning

Unit 14: Family Business and Governance

📖 Core Concepts
  • Family businesses = organizations where ownership and management are concentrated in a family
  • Key challenges: Succession planning, Family vs Business conflict, Professionalization, Governance issues
  • Coping strategies: Clear governance structure, Professional management, Family constitution, Conflict resolution, Merit-based promotions

💡 Real-World Example

Reliance Industries — Dhirubhai Ambani founded, Mukesh & Anil took over. The split demonstrated both succession challenges and governance issues. Tata Group = better governance model with professional management + family oversight.

📐 Case Study: Tata vs Reliance — Governance Contrast

Tata Group: Professional management through Tata Sons, family oversight through charitable trusts, smooth succession (Ratan Tata → N. Chandrasekaran). Result: ₹15+ lakh crore empire with stability.

Reliance: Dhirubhai's succession led to bitter split between Mukesh and Anil Ambani. No formal family constitution. Result: Reliance Industries thrived (Mukesh), but Anil's ventures struggled. Demonstrates why governance structures matter.

🧠 Memory Aid

"S-F-P-G-C" — Succession, Family conflict, Professionalization, Governance, Constitution

🎯 Exam Tip

Succession planning is the #1 topic in family business questions. Compare Reliance (conflict) vs Tata (smooth governance) as contrasting examples.

✅ Unit Summary

  • 4 challenges: Succession, Family-Business conflict, Professionalization, Governance
  • 5 strategies: Governance structure, Professional management, Family constitution, Conflict resolution, Merit-based promotions
  • Compare Reliance (conflict) vs Tata (smooth governance)
