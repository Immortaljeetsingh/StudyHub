Unit 1: An Overview of Retail Sector

Retailing = sale of goods/services to end customer for personal consumption. Retailers break bulk from manufacturers into small quantities. India's retail = 8-10% of GDP, 2nd largest employer after agriculture. Unorganized sector = 88% (12 million+ small retailers). Brick and Click = combining physical stores with onli.

Wheel of Retailing: New formats enter as low-price, low-margin, low-status → upgrade over time → become vulnerable to new low-price entrants. Merchandising = product assortment planning + visual displays. Planograms = shelf space allocation diagrams.

Unit 3: Retail Environment

Shrinkage = inventory loss from theft, damage, or errors. India's rate: 2.38% (~INR 3,470 crores). Shoplifting = 47.6%, Staff theft = 25.5%. Greentailing = environmentally friendly retailing.
