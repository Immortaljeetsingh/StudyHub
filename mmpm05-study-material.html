Unit 1: Marketing of Services — An Introduction

Service (Kotler): "Any activity or benefit that one party can offer to another that is essentially intangible and does not result in the ownership of anything."

Unit 2: Conceptual Framework for Services Marketing

Traditional 4Ps (Product, Price, Place, Promotion) are insufficient for services. Extended to 7Ps with addition of People, Process, Physical Evidence. Service Marketing Triangle — 3 types of marketing must align: External (Company → Customer), Internal (Company → Employee), Interactive (Employee → Customer).

Unit 3: Consumer Behaviour in Services

Service consumers evaluate based on expectations vs. perceptions . Three types of service qualities determine how consumers evaluate. Zone of Tolerance = range of acceptable service levels between desired service and adequate service. Wider for less important attributes; narrows when service is critical.

Services pricing is harder than goods pricing due to intangibility. Three basic methods: Cost-based (cost + markup), Competition-based (match/beat competitors), Value-based (customer perceived value).

Unit 7: Promotion Decisions

Intangibility makes service communication challenging. Must "tangibilize the intangible" through tangible cues, testimonials, guarantees. Challenges: Cannot show service before purchase; must rely on tangible cues; need to manage expectations (avoid overpromising).

Unit 8: Managing People

People = 5th P. Service employees are "boundary spanners" between company and customer. Emotional labour = displaying emotions not genuinely felt. Internal Marketing: Treat employees as internal customers. Train, motivate, and empower them.
